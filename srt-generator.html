Export formats

Vosuba exports all three major subtitle formats from a single project.

SRT (SubRip Text)

The universal standard. Works with YouTube, Vimeo, Facebook, Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, and virtually every video player and platform.

VTT (WebVTT)

The web-native format. Used by HTML5 video players, web-based LMS platforms, and streaming services. Supports styling hints and positioning.

ASS (Advanced SSA)

The professional format. Full typographic control — fonts, colours, positioning, and effects. Imports natively into DaVinci Resolve and Aegisub.

Frequently asked questions

What is an SRT file?

SRT (SubRip Text) is the most widely supported subtitle format. It stores timed text segments with sequence numbers, timestamps, and caption text. SRT files can be uploaded to YouTube, Vimeo, and most social media platforms, or imported into video editors like Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve.

Is Vosuba's SRT generator really free?

Yes. The free tier of Vosuba includes unlimited AI transcription and SRT export — no watermark on text files, no per-minute fees, no account required. The paid tiers (Creator $49, Pro $99) add styled visual captions, burn-in, voiceover, and batch processing.

What video formats does the SRT generator support?

Vosuba accepts MP4, MOV, MKV, AVI, WEBM, and most other video and audio formats via FFmpeg. You can also import standalone audio files (MP3, WAV, M4A) to generate subtitle files from podcasts or voice recordings.

Can I generate SRT files for YouTube?

Yes. Vosuba exports standard .srt files that are directly compatible with YouTube Studio's subtitle upload feature. You can also export VTT files for web players or ASS files for professional editors like DaVinci Resolve and Final Cut Pro.
