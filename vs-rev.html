The bottom line

Use Rev if…

You need human-verified transcription for legal, medical, or broadcast content where 99%+ accuracy is non-negotiable — and you're willing to pay $1.50/min for it. Rev is also a good fit if you're on Windows or need an API for automated workflows.

Use Vosuba if…

You need unlimited subtitle generation without per-minute costs. Vosuba is the better choice for creators, editors, and institutions who caption regularly — it pays for itself after just 200 minutes of use, runs offline, and adds styled captions, burn-in, WCAG compliance, and AI voiceover that Rev doesn't offer at any price.

Common questions

Is Rev or Vosuba cheaper for subtitles?

It depends on volume. Rev's AI transcription costs $0.25 per minute. At that rate, 200 minutes of video costs $50 — more than Vosuba Creator's one-time $49 price for unlimited transcription. If you regularly subtitle content, Vosuba pays for itself after your first 200 minutes and every minute after that is free.

Is Rev's human transcription more accurate than Vosuba?

Rev offers human transcription at $1.50/min which is highly accurate (99%+ claimed). Vosuba uses OpenAI's Whisper model locally, which typically achieves under 5% word error rate on clear audio. For most subtitle workflows, AI accuracy is sufficient — and you can edit every segment in Vosuba's built-in editor before export.

Does Rev work offline?

No. Rev is a cloud-only service — you upload your audio or video files to Rev's servers for processing. Vosuba runs entirely on your Mac with no internet connection required. Your footage never leaves your machine.

Can Vosuba replace Rev for YouTube subtitles?

Yes. Vosuba generates SRT and VTT files from any video — the same formats Rev outputs. The difference is Vosuba does it locally with no per-minute cost, and adds caption styling, burn-in, WCAG compliance checking, and AI voiceover on top. For YouTube creators who regularly caption videos, Vosuba eliminates ongoing transcription fees.
