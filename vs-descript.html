Vosuba vs Descript
Offline subtitle studio vs cloud transcript editor

Descript edits video by editing text in the cloud. Vosuba styles, burns, and exports subtitles entirely on your Mac. Here's when to use each — and why the pricing models are worlds apart.

Quick answer: Vosuba is an offline Mac subtitle studio ($49–$99 one-time) while Descript is a cloud-based transcript video editor ($24–$33/mo). Vosuba processes everything locally with no uploads, exports SRT/VTT/ASS, includes WCAG compliance tools and AI voiceover. Descript offers full video editing via transcript but requires internet and costs $288–$396/year. Vosuba saves 75–87% over 2 years.

The bottom line

Use Descript if…

You need a full video editor that uses transcript-based editing. Descript excels at removing filler words, cutting clips by deleting text, screen recording, and collaborative team workflows. It's built for creators who edit long-form content and want an all-in-one cloud platform.

Use Vosuba if…

You need professional subtitles without uploading your footage. Vosuba is the better choice for styled captions, WCAG compliance, ASS export for DaVinci Resolve, AI voiceover, and batch processing — all running offline on your Mac with a single payment instead of a monthly subscription.

Common questions

Is Vosuba a Descript alternative?

Yes, for subtitle-focused workflows. Vosuba covers transcription, styled captions, burn-in, WCAG compliance, and AI voiceover — all offline on Mac. Descript is a broader cloud-based video editor that uses transcript-based editing. If you primarily need subtitles and voiceovers without uploading footage, Vosuba is the stronger offline alternative.

Does Descript work offline?

No. Descript requires an internet connection for transcription, AI features, and project syncing. All media is processed on Descript's cloud servers. Vosuba runs 100% offline on your Mac — no internet required after the initial download.

Which is cheaper, Vosuba or Descript?

Vosuba is a one-time purchase starting at $49 (Creator) or $99 (Pro with voiceover). Descript charges $24/month (Hobbyist) or $33/month (Professional), which totals $288–$396 per year. Over two years, Vosuba costs 75–87% less than Descript.

Can Vosuba replace Descript for YouTube subtitles?

Yes, if your primary workflow is adding subtitles to videos. Vosuba transcribes, styles, and exports SRT/VTT files for YouTube upload, or burns captions directly into your video — all without a subscription or cloud upload. For full video editing (cuts, transitions, effects), Descript offers more, but it requires a monthly subscription.
